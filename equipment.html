Equipment and Systems

1–18 of 4,000+ by most popular

Norvane ChillersPremium

Centrifugal and screw chillers for campuses and towers. Read approach temperatures, stage compressors and reset supply water.

BACnet Devices

Any controller that speaks BACnet/IP or MS/TP. Discover points, map them once and reuse the map on every site.

Halcyon VAV

Variable air volume boxes with reheat. Trim airflow zone by zone and follow occupancy instead of a timetable.

Orrin Heat Pumps

Air and ground source heat pumps. Pre-heat on cheap hours and hold the defrost cycle away from your peak.

Modbus Meters

Generic Modbus TCP and RTU meters. Pull interval data every fifteen seconds and tag it by tenant or circuit.

Fennick Air Handlers

Packaged and built-up air handling units. Reset static pressure, schedule purge and watch filter loading.

Tallow BoilersPremium

Condensing boiler plants. Sequence firing, hold return water low and catch short cycling early.

Lumio Lighting

Networked lighting panels and drivers. Dim on daylight, sweep floors at night and share occupancy with HVAC.

Presq Sensors

Ceiling and desk sensors that count people without cameras. Use counts to set ventilation and cleaning.

Coldharbor CasesBeta

Supermarket display cases. Float suction pressure, stagger defrost and prove food stayed safe.

Gridwell BatteriesPremium

Commercial battery systems. Shave demand peaks, stack tariffs and keep a reserve for outages.

Deskline Tickets

Facilities help desk. Turn a hot or cold call into a diagnosed ticket with the trend already attached.

Solace Inverters

String and central inverters. Compare yield with irradiance and flag soiling before it costs you.

Hallway Chat

Team messaging. Send alarms where engineers already talk and let them acknowledge in one tap.

Meridian Water

Pulse and ultrasonic water meters. Find overnight flow, split leaks from irrigation and bill fairly.

Kestrel Access

Door and badge systems. Wake floors on first entry and release them when the last person leaves.

Umbra ShadesBeta

Motorised blinds and facade louvres. Cut solar gain before the chiller has to remove it.

Pellworth Hot Water

Storage and instantaneous water heaters. Shift the tank, run legionella cycles and log every one.

MQTT Streams

Publish and subscribe to any MQTT broker. Bring custom sensors in without writing a driver.

Northgate GatewaysPremium

Small edge boxes that sit beside the old controller. Buffer ninety days of data if the line drops.

Frostline RoomsBeta

Walk-in cold rooms and blast chillers. Track door openings and core temperature side by side.

Aurel Fan Coils

Two and four pipe fan coils for hotels. Follow check-in, key card and window contacts.

Requests is a simple way to collect comfort calls and hand them to routines that can reach every system you own.
