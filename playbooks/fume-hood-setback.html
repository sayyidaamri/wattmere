Fume hood setback

Lower airflow with a proof log. Ready in an afternoon with equipment you already own.

Overview

Fume hood setback is a ready-made routine for commissioning. Lower airflow with a proof log. It suits facilities and energy teams who want a repeatable result without writing a sequence from scratch, and it leaves a clear record of each step.

Key features

  • Live readings: Works from current points, with quality and age checked before anything moves.
  • Adjustable limits: Choose bands by site, system or zone, and change them without touching the routine.
  • Clear notices: The right person hears once, with the trend attached.
  • Works together: Reads from meters, sensors and calendars you have already connected.

Why teams use it

  • Less repeat work: The common case is handled, so people see the exceptions.
  • Better answers: Occupants get a reply that says what was done and why.
  • Lasting record: Each run is stored beside the readings that caused it.

How to use it

  1. Connect: Pick the site and confirm the suggested points.
  2. Set limits: Accept our starting bands or enter your own.
  3. Replay: Run it on last week and compare.
  4. Release: Turn it on for one zone, then the rest.

Ways to adapt it

  • Add approval: Ask an engineer before the first write of the day.
  • Add a season: Use different limits in summer and winter.
  • Add a report: Send a monthly page to whoever pays the bill.

Start small, read the log after a week, and widen it when you trust it.
