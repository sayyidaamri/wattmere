How Osprey Health tests a sequence in hours, not shutdowns

to prove a new sequence, down from a weekend

osprey
Company size9,400 employees

“Wattmere made life quieter for our plant team. We can test a new sequence in an afternoon instead of waiting for a shutdown.”

Elena Ortiz, Head of Estates Operations

Seeing the building clearly

Challenge

Before Wattmere, the team behind osprey relied on vendor screens, spreadsheets and memory. Each site told its own version of events, and nothing lined up with the bill.

Solution

Readings now land in one Ledger with shared names. Limits were agreed once with safety and finance, and written into the platform.

Results

The team can answer a question about any system in minutes, and spends its time on the few that matter.

From one site to all of them

Challenge

A good sequence used to stay where it was written. Moving it meant another contractor and another visit.

Solution

The team replayed the sequence on each site’s own data, adjusted the bands and released it a zone at a time.

Results

2 hours to prove a new sequence, down from a weekend, with every step on record.

Trust, kept in a log

Challenge

Operators worried about losing control, and leadership worried about the opposite.

Solution

Every change carries a name, a reason and a release timer. Anyone can stop a routine with one click.

Results

13% less reheat energy across two hospitals.

“What matters most to my team is that the advanced things are easy to do. Someone a few weeks in can write a routine, replay it and release it. We do not need a room full of specialists, we need the right tools.”

Elena Ortiz, Head of Estates Operations
